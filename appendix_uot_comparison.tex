\section{Fixed-Size UOT Comparison}
\label{app:uot-fixed-size}

\paragraph{Time, memory, and accuracy.}
We first compare \ours with \mm and \sinkhorn at a common problem size, reporting runtime, peak GPU memory, and \sharedgap in Table~\ref{tab:uot-comparison}.
The four penalty settings cover weak, moderate, and strong symmetric penalties, together with a highly asymmetric configuration.
The \sinkhorn baseline uses $\varepsilon=10^{-3}$.
